About & Statement

Soroor Kheirollahi is a contemporary Iranian painter working within the visual language of Surrealism. Her paintings explore inner psychological landscapes, identity, and the poetic tension between memory and subconscious forms.

She holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Painting from Islamic Azad University, Abadan Branch, and currently lives and works in Mahshahr, Iran.
