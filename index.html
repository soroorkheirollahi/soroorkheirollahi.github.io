About & Statement

Soroor Kheirollahi is an Iranian visual artist and painter specializing in Surrealism. Through psychological metaphors and subconscious imagery, her work explores internal identities, human emotion, and poetic transformations between reality and dream.

She received her Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Painting from Islamic Azad University, Abadan Branch (2014) and is an active member of the Khuzestan Visual Arts Association. She currently lives and works in Mahshahr, Iran.
