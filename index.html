Artist Statement & Biography

Soroor Kheirollahi (b. Iran) is a contemporary visual artist focused on surrealist painting. Her visual vocabulary investigates the friction between personal identity, inner psyche, and external transformations. Employing deliberate distortion, psychological depth, and atmospheric balance, her works navigate liminal spaces between memory and subconscious observation.

She holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Painting from Islamic Azad University, Abadan (graduated with high distinction, 2014) and has actively contributed to the regional contemporary art scene as an educator and active member of the Khuzestan Visual Arts Association.
